See me die

by Charles Master · · 2 min read · 34 lines

As I Walk through all the world

A vessel to fill you and your gods

Let me pour you my dried blood, you vampires

As my bones start to fall, and am sick

But you see all, am the dead, long dead

Heartbeat yes, it's not life but a big fall for

I don't have the muscle, no hope, no joy, no life

See my moving corpse, your god betrayed

Me know that am dead, and I got no chance and

There is no forgiveness, for my eyes were opened

And you said I am wise, but I know I am the ghost

And you pitied when I suffered, already dead

Never knew, never see, that this soft hand

Writing. The owner died long before his soul vanished

But you say I must hope, yet the hope is

The poison that ruined me, say there is a reason

A reason to live is but tears and pains and losses

And more years of pains and losses and fears

A little more years of hope in vain and pain again

A little mind paradise, I will watch you disappointed

A small hopeful lie that happiness comes slowly

But how can I feel it am already dead!!!?

You think me crazy? I wish I wasn't, but

Then crazy to speak the deadly truth always

Or you wish me back to life, or you want me

Fill you till me empty, alone, sadist and more dead

And when I rise again, your gods know me immortal

Yet am really dead, but their greed is severely total

Then you hold my hands, comfort me, eased

But did you see litres I weep every night? Girl.

I lost my life but the body keeps moving

They lie and you keep believing

Me dead, you keep blindly hoping

I guess I must be buried to prove...

— TheLongAfter
